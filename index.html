About Me

Hi, I'm Maryam.

Product Designer based in Nigeria. I design AI, fintech, edtech and climate products for people in emerging markets, from learning platforms to marketplaces. I'm currently the founder and product lead at EcoSmart AI. Previously, I spent two years as the product designer on Carmpus, a learning management system that grew into an AI adaptive learning platform at CodeBoulevard Systems.

As a founder and a designer, I fall in love with the problem before the solution. I've sat with recyclers who lost money to bad deals, and with market women keeping a whole group's savings in a notebook. Their problems shape everything I design.

I don't just build with AI, I design how AI behaves: when it should admit it's unsure, when it should stay out of the way, and how people can correct it. And I keep testing until people trust it.

I work closely with developers, from early ideas to handoff, so what I design is what ships. When I'm not designing products, I'm illustrating. See that side of me on Instagram.
