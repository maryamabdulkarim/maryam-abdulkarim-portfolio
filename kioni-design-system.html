Kioni Design System

A calm, tactile design system crafted for a private career journal — 100% WCAG AA contrast, light & dark token parity, and comprehensive component states.

System Scope

Design tokens, typography scales, tactile paper surfaces, component anatomy, light/dark themes, and accessibility contrast matrices.

Scale & Coverage

66 artboards, 22 core screens, 7 interaction states per component, 11/11 WCAG AA passing color pairs.

Tactile Metaphor

White cards hold interactive controls; warm cream paper holds what you wrote about yourself. Gold signals wins and items needing a look.

Tools Used

Figma (design tokens, component variants, auto layout), Claude (design exploration, copywriting, accessibility audits).

Design System Cover

"Tactile foundations for private reflection"

The Kioni design system was built from the ground up to support thoughtful, stress-free reflection. Instead of aggressive notifications or gamified streaks, every component, card, and surface conveys permanence and trust.

System Principles

"Five principles decided every token"

Every token, radius, padding, and interactive state in the system was derived from our five core product principles:

⛶ View full screen

Principles Board: Direct mapping from product ethos to concrete UI constraints

01

Private by default

Nothing leaves Kioni unless you copy, print or download it. Every screen says who can see what.

02

Your words, kept as you wrote them

Anything you write about yourself sits on cream "paper". Kioni never rewrites it, and edits keep a history.

03

Calm, not gamified

No scores, no streak shaming, no red badges. Every result counts, including "Not yet".

04

Small steps, real work

Thirty seconds on a Friday is enough. One question at a time, and anything extra is marked optional.

05

Honest by design

Limits, prices and consequences are shown before you act, never after.

Accessibility Standards

"100% WCAG AA contrast compliance"

We rigorously tested every text, border, and background pairing across both light and dark modes. All 11 primary and secondary color combinations comfortably exceed the 4.5:1 WCAG AA minimum threshold for normal text and 3:1 for large text and interactive components.
