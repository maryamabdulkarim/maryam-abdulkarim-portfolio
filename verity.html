The Idea

The idea

Most AI detectors give one unclear verdict. Verity splits it in two: a Trust Score (how credible the content is) and a Confidence Score (how sure the AI is), always with the evidence behind them.

01

Trust Score

Shows how credible the image, text, or document is on a 0–100 scale paired with one of five clear trust levels.

02

Confidence Score + Evidence

Separates how certain the model is from the credibility rating itself, and lists the exact checks behind the result.

Design System

Design system

Two brand greens (#063F25 and #5EBB46) anchor the interface across light and dark mode, paired with five colour-coded trust levels—every foreground and background pair checked for WCAG contrast.
